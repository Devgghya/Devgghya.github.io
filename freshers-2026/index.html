Dress code / theme

Come as Bollywood.
Not as yourself.

Recreate a specific movie character, a 90s/2000s look, a filmy hero or diva, a red-carpet vibe, or even a Bollywood meme. The goal is recognizable + fun, not expensive or exact.

Quick answers

Still overthinking the outfit?

Do I have to buy a new Bollywood costume?
No. Use clothes you already own, borrow from friends or siblings, and add one or two recognizable details like sunglasses, a scarf, dupatta, bandana or jewellery.
Does my outfit need to be an exact movie copy?
Not at all. Recreate the key details or simply match the character's vibe. If people can understand the reference, it works.
Can I recreate a meme instead of an actor?
Absolutely. Bollywood memes, iconic scenes and funny recreations are part of the theme.
Can friends coordinate matching characters?
Yes — duos, trios and group recreations are encouraged.
